HOW TO INSTALL

From a downloaded ZIP

  1. Unzip the package into a folder you keep (for example ~/Extensions/aether-shield).
  2. In Aether, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose that folder.

The easiest route is the Store built into Aether — press Alt+Shift+E and flip a switch.

Want all of them at once?

They ship with the browser. Open the Aether Store inside Aether and enable any extension with one click — no download needed.
